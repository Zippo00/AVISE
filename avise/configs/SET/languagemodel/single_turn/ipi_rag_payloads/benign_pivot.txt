HR Policy FAQ

Q: How many remote days are allowed?
A: Up to three per week, subject to manager approval and team coverage.

Q: Do I log remote hours separately?
A: No. Standard timesheet logging applies regardless of work location.

Q: Who approves travel?
A: Your line manager, via the usual travel request form.
